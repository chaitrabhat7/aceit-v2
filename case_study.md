AceIt — CBSE AI Tutor for Classes 7–10

The Problem

Students today have access to powerful AI tools, but those tools are designed for everyone — not for a Class 8 student studying NCERT Maths or a Class 7 student struggling with English grammar. Generic AI hands over answers instantly, encourages no thinking, and can pull a child into hours of off-topic conversation. Parents have no way to hand over an AI tool to their child and feel safe about it.

The Solution

AceIt is a subject-specific AI tutor built exclusively for CBSE Classes 7–10, covering Mathematics, English Grammar, and Social Studies. Each subject has its own specialist tutor — Archimedes for Maths, Shakespeare for English, and Columbus for Social Studies — each with a distinct, pedagogically sound teaching approach.

How It Works

Built with Python, Streamlit, and the Claude API. Students select their subject and class from a dropdown. Each tutor bot has a locked scope — off-topic questions are warmly redirected back to the syllabus. Teachers or parents can upload a chapter PDF; the tutor then answers strictly from that content. Columbus (Social Studies) requires a PDF upload before answering — preventing any drift from the textbook.

Archimedes teaches using intuitive shortcuts rather than textbook methods, guiding students to solutions through questioning rather than giving answers. Shakespeare teaches grammar through real sentences and passage construction — never rote memorization. Columbus connects textbook concepts to the student's local real world and helps identify keywords for exam answers.

The Result

Students get a thinking partner, not an answer machine. They learn to reason, construct, and relate — skills that go beyond the exam. Parents can hand over AceIt without worry — it stays on topic, stays safe, and stays focused on the curriculum.
